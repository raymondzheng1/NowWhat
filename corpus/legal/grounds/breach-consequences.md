id: breach-consequences
name: Consequences of a breach
plainName: Does breaking the rule make the decision invalid?
oneLine: Not every mistake makes a decision invalid.
whatItMeans: >-
  Sometimes a rule was not followed. The next question is what that means. Some
  mistakes make a decision invalid. Others do not. It depends on what the Act
  intended, and on whether the mistake could have changed the outcome.
plainExample: >-
  An office missed a step it was meant to follow. Whether that undoes the
  decision depends on the Act.
whatRelates:
  - A step in the process was skipped.
  - You were not given something the rules say you get.
  - A time frame in the rules was not kept to.
  - Something went wrong that might have changed the answer.
whatItIsNot: >-
  This is not a ground on its own. It is the question that follows once
  something has gone wrong.
usedIn:
  - judicial-review
test: >-
  Whether a mistake makes a decision invalid depends on the purpose of the Act,
  and on whether the mistake could have made a difference.

undoes the decision is a separate matter, and one a free legal service is best placed to
