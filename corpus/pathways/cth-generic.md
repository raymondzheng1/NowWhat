The Commonwealth catch-all. Chosen when a letter is clearly from an Australian Government
body but matches no specific guide — an NDIA, DVA, ATO, Medicare, Home Affairs or child
support decision. Before this existed, those letters matched nothing and the person was told
we did not cover them.

Content reviewed and approved by the owner on 2026-08-17.

Three decisions taken when the draft was finalised, recorded so a later reader does not have
to guess:

**The judicial-review provision is the ADJR Act, s 5.** Three provisions could carry it —
ADJR s 5, Judiciary Act s 39B, or Constitution s 75(v). Naming all three is accurate and
useless to someone reading at a year-6 level. The ADJR Act is the ordinary route, and the
owner's own materials describe it as the more straightforward and less technical one.

**The internal-review pathway was dropped.** Most Commonwealth schemes have one, but they
differ so much — NDIS internal review, DVA review, ATO objection, child support objection —
that no general statement could be sourced. A catch-all that gestures vaguely at a step
somebody may or may not have is worse than one that does not mention it. Per-scheme entries
should carry it; this entry should not.

**No time limit appears anywhere, deliberately.** Limits are set by each enabling Act and
there is no general Commonwealth figure. `deadlineRule` in the procedural layer says a limit
applies and to confirm it, which is the whole of what can honestly be said here.
